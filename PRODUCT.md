# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women in Bratislava and the surrounding area (Most pri Bratislave, Senec, Ivanka pri Dunaji, Pezinok) who want premium **hair extensions** (predlžovanie / zahusťovanie vlasov) or **reconstruction & regeneration treatments** (rekonštrukčné a regeneračné kúry).

This is a considered, higher-value, emotional purchase — it's about confidence and self-image, not a routine trim. Visitors are typically researching on a phone, comparing a few salons, and looking for two things before they commit: proof that the person doing the work is a genuine specialist, and a sense that they'll be personally cared for rather than processed.

The job to be done: find a trusted specialist, understand the methods and roughly what they cost, see real results, and book — by phone — with confidence.

## Product Purpose

A single-page marketing site that positions **O VLASY by Zane** (ovlasy.sk; formerly Good Hair by Zane) as a specialist boutique for hair *health* — regeneration treatments, Malibu C deep cleansing of scalp and hair, and hairstyles for special occasions. Hair extensions are no longer offered (dropped in the 2026-09 rebrand). The brand manifesto — "O vlasy sa staráme. O vlasy, nie iba o účes. O vlasy s rozumom." — is the voice; section headings play on it — explicitly **not** a general kaderníctvo. Its job is to turn a first-time visitor into a phone booking.

Success looks like: qualified inquiries and bookings, and a first impression that feels premium *and* personally warm — enough that a visitor trusts Zane with a significant, personal investment in how they look.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **warm, expert, boutique.**

The voice is personal and caring — it speaks one-to-one to each client ("individuálny prístup ku každej jednej z vás"), confident about its craft without bragging. The emotional target is that a visitor feels *welcomed and personally looked after* while clearly sensing high-end quality. Warmth and luxury are not in tension here; warmth is *how* this brand does luxury.

## Anti-references

- **The generic local-salon ("kaderníctvo") template** — interchangeable stock photos, default theme layouts, the look every neighbourhood salon site has. If a section could belong to any salon, it's wrong.
- **Anything that reads cheap or discount** — loud promo banners, clutter, bargain signalling, "sale" energy.
- **Cold, clinical, or corporate sterility** — sterile medical/SaaS feel, no warmth, no human presence.

## Design Principles

1. **Boutique, never template.** Every section should feel made for Zane specifically. The test: if it could be dropped into any salon site unchanged, redo it.
2. **Warmth carries the luxury.** The premium feel comes from generous space, restraint, real craft, and genuine care — not from gloss, effects, or gradients-everywhere. This luxury whispers and welcomes; it does not shout.
3. **Specialist authority, made legible.** Surface the expertise — the treatments and what they solve (hard water, chlorine, build-up, irritated scalp), 10+ years, real results — clearly and reassuringly, so a considered, emotional purchase feels safe.
4. **One-to-one, not one-to-many.** Copy, imagery, and tone address each client personally. Never transactional, never "processed".
5. **Show the work.** Real results and the real space do more than adjectives. Let photography and proof carry the persuasion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Slovak-language (sk) throughout. Content keywords carry SEO weight, so copy must read naturally to humans first.
- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**: body text ≥ 4.5:1 against its background; large/bold text ≥ 3:1. Body text was recently darkened (#8A7F72 → #5C4A35) toward this — pairs still need verifying.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` (already implemented globally); every animation needs a reduced-motion fallback.
- Mobile-first: most visitors arrive on phones. Tap targets, performance, and legibility on small screens take priority. Expensive GPU effects are already disabled below 1024px.
- Keyboard-accessible interactive elements; the phone number is always a clickable `tel:` link.
